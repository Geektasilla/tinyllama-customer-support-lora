# Customer account

## Resetting a forgotten password
Click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your email address.
You receive a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes.
If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder.

## Deleting an account
Go to My Account → Settings → Privacy and click "Delete account".
The account and personal data are deleted within 30 days, as required by the GDPR.
Invoices are kept for 10 years because German tax law requires it.

## Newsletter
You can unsubscribe from the newsletter with the link at the end of every newsletter
or in My Account → Settings → Notifications.
