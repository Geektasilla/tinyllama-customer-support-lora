# Returns and refunds

## Returning an item
Items can be returned within 30 days after delivery.
Returns are free: create a return label in My Account → Returns and drop the parcel at any DHL point.
Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

## Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, opened hygiene products and personalised items cannot be returned.

## Refunds
The refund is issued within 5 business days after the returned items arrive at our warehouse.
The money goes back to the original payment method.
You receive an email as soon as the refund is issued.
