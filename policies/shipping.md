# Shipping and delivery

## Shipping costs and delivery times in Germany
Standard delivery within Germany takes 2–4 business days and costs €4.90.
Shipping is free for orders of €50 or more.
Express delivery costs €9.90 and arrives on the next business day if the order is placed before 14:00.
We also deliver to DHL Packstation lockers.

## Shipping to other EU countries
We ship to all EU countries. Delivery takes 4–7 business days and costs €12.90.
Free shipping to other EU countries starts at €100.
We do not ship outside the European Union.

## Tracking a package
As soon as the order is shipped, we send an email with a tracking link.
The tracking link is also shown in My Account → Orders.
If the tracking status has not changed for 5 business days, contact Customer Support and we will open an investigation with the carrier.
