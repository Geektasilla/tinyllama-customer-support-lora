# Payments

## Payment methods
We accept PayPal, Visa, Mastercard, SEPA direct debit and Klarna invoice.
We do not accept cash on delivery.

## Paying by invoice (Klarna)
With Klarna invoice you pay within 14 days after the order is shipped.
Klarna sends the payment details by email.

## Charged twice
If a payment appears twice, one of the entries is usually only a pending authorisation.
Pending authorisations disappear automatically within 5 business days.
If both amounts are still charged after 5 business days, contact Customer Support with the order number and we will refund the duplicate payment.
